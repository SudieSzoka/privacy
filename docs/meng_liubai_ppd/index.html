LIUBAI · WEBSITE BLOCKER

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026 · Extension version: 1.0.2

This policy describes the Chrome extension Liubai — Website Blocker (留白 · 网站屏蔽), product ID meng_liubai_ppd. Developer contact: hzaulpl@gmail.com.

Your blocking list stays on your device. Optional sign-in and membership payments are handled through ShipLang. Account and membership records are processed separately from your local blocking list; the extension does not upload that list or your tab addresses.

1. Information processed

  • Blocking preferences: domain names you add, the global blocking switch, individual site switches, and whether subdomains are included.
  • Tab and link addresses: the extension reads the active tab address, checks open tabs and navigation addresses, and reads a page or link address when you choose its context-menu action. These addresses are processed locally to identify domains and apply blocking.
  • Trial and membership: a first-use timestamp and remaining trial-operation count are saved locally. After sign-in, the extension also saves the account identifier, product identifier, email address, membership expiration timestamp, and any name or avatar returned by the account service.
  • Imported files: when you explicitly select a JSON or text file, the extension reads it locally and merges valid domains into your list. Importing a URL stores its domain, not its path or query.

The extension does not read website page contents, passwords, cookies, form entries, or payment details. It does not request access to your browser's stored browsing-history database.

2. Local storage and blocked-page addresses

Preferences and domains are saved in chrome.storage.local. Blocking rules are kept by Chrome through its declarative network request API. The extension does not use Chrome storage sync or upload these settings to a server.

When a tab is redirected to the extension's local blocked page, its original URL is included in that page's URL fragment so the original domain can be displayed. The fragment can include the original path, query, or fragment. It is not sent by this extension to a server, but the local page URL may remain visible in the address bar or be retained by Chrome's tab/session features. Close the tab or follow your browser's controls to clear it. The extension does not maintain a separate browsing-history log.

3. Why permissions are needed

  • storage: save blocking preferences and the list on your device.
  • tabs: identify the current website, check and redirect matching open tabs, and react to navigation.
  • contextMenus: offer a command to block a page or link from its right-click menu.
  • declarativeNetRequest: redirect matching top-level website requests to the local blocked page.
  • alarms: recheck trial and membership expiration and suspend blocking when access expires, even when the popup is closed.
  • Access to HTTP and HTTPS websites: apply user-selected blocking rules across arbitrary domains and subdomains. The rules target main-frame navigation; this access is not used to read page content or upload browsing information.

4. Sign-in, payments, and third-party services

Sign-in and checkout open external account pages through https://crx.shiplang.com. On opening those pages, the extension sends the product AppId, Chrome extension ID, extension version, browser interface language, and, when available, your current account identifier and locally stored membership expiration. The service returns account and membership information to a local extension receiver. The extension accepts results only from the expected ShipLang origin, service window, and matching product. Blocking lists, tab addresses, and imported files are not included in these account requests.

ShipLang provides account and payment infrastructure. Its integration guidance describes retaining the email, avatar, and membership expiration needed for account service until a deletion request, without retaining backups. To request deletion, email support@shiplang.com with this extension's name or download link and your request; that guidance specifies processing within 7 days. A local sign-out removes the extension's saved account record, and does not delete the remote account or cancel a subscription. The developer and ShipLang are independent; this product does not represent ShipLang.

Google sign-in is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. Payment details are entered on the checkout provider's site, rather than stored by this extension. The payment options and provider shown on checkout determine the applicable terms: if checkout uses Stripe, see the Stripe Privacy Policy; if it uses PayPro Global, see the PayPro Global Privacy Policy. Follow the checkout page for actual pricing, membership terms, and payment options.

While an account is signed in, the extension configures an uninstall feedback link to ShipLang containing the account identifier, extension version, and interface language. Chrome opens that link if you uninstall. Signing out clears this link. This does not send your blocking list.

Opening external account, checkout, support, or policy links makes normal requests to their destination services, which can process connection data such as IP address, browser information, and service cookies under their policies. This public policy is hosted on GitHub Pages; see the GitHub General Privacy Statement. Support email is processed by the relevant recipient and email providers. Do not send passwords, access tokens, or private browsing lists.

5. Import, export, and user control

You can add, remove, enable, or disable domains from the management page, and pause blocking globally. Pausing blocking retains your saved list. Removing a domain updates the local list and rules. The extension has no built-in cloud backup or automatic sync of your list.

Simplified Chinese is free without trial limits. Other interface languages have a trial lasting 7 days from first use, with up to 10 successful add/import operations. One successful batch import counts as one operation; failures, cancellations, and imports with no new sites do not consume operations. After the quota is used, adding/importing requires membership, while existing rules continue until the 7-day deadline. After that deadline without active membership, blocking is suspended and the list is preserved. Removal, export, and account controls remain available. An active membership removes these trial limits. Reopening or updating does not reset the local trial.

Export creates a JSON file containing your domain list and site settings. The file is saved through your browser to a location you control. It remains on your device after uninstalling the extension; delete exported files separately when no longer needed. Imported source files are not modified or deleted by the extension.

6. Retention and deletion

Local preferences and trial records remain until you clear the extension's local storage through browser controls or uninstall the extension; individual domains can also be removed in the management page. Saved account details remain locally until you sign out, clear the extension's storage, or uninstall. Remote account deletion must be requested from ShipLang as described above. Uninstalling removes the extension's local data and blocking rules through Chrome. Browser backups, tab/session records, exported copies, and email correspondence are controlled separately. The developer cannot remotely view or erase your local blocking list.

7. Changes to this policy

If a future release changes data handling, including changing account/payment handling or adding sync or analytics, this policy will be updated to describe that behavior. The current policy applies to the version stated above.
