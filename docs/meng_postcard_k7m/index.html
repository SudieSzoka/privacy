POSTCARD · WEB TEXT TO IMAGES

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026 · Extension version 2.0.2

This policy covers Postcard: Web Text to Images, product ID meng_postcard_k7m. Developer contact: rcchenfeng@gmail.com.

Webpage text is processed locally to create image cards. The extension does not upload card content or source links. Optional sign-in and membership checkout use ShipLang. Account information is handled separately from your cards.

1. Information used

  • When you invoke capture, the extension reads the text block, article, post, or comment you click, or text you select on the page, including visible text, title, author, source URL, platform, and any available date. It supports text capture on ordinary HTTP and HTTPS webpages, with dedicated adapters for X, Reddit, LinkedIn, Threads, Bluesky, and Hacker News. Browser-restricted pages cannot be captured. It does not automatically expand hidden text or fetch original media.
  • You may manually enter and edit card text, author, and source URL. Anonymization hides the author and source in the exported image. It does not redact names, handles, or other identifiers written inside the title or body. Review your card before sharing.
  • The extension stores design preferences and a trial export count locally. It does not use Chrome storage sync.
  • If you sign in, a local account record may contain your account identifier, product identifier, email, name, avatar URL, and membership expiration returned by the service. The extension does not read passwords, browser cookies, form entries, or the browser's stored history database.

2. Storage and retention

After a successful capture, the extension saves up to 12 structural region rules per website origin in local storage. These rules contain selectors, element types, and child tag names; they do not contain the captured text, author, or source URL. They are used locally to detect similar regions under your pointer and display a capture button. Website origins and these rules remain until extension data is cleared or the extension is uninstalled. Revoking website permission disables future automatic injection but does not delete the local rules.

Card drafts are held in chrome.storage.session and in the editor's memory. They are not uploaded or synchronized. Session storage is cleared when the browser session ends, the extension is disabled or reloaded, or the extension is removed. Older session drafts are also removed when a new card is opened after 30 minutes without editing. A card already displayed in an open editor may remain in that tab until it closes. Clear card removes that editor's content and replaces its session draft after a short save delay.

Theme, layout, resolution, typeface, text size, attribution switches, and trial usage are stored in chrome.storage.local until you clear extension storage or uninstall. Signing out removes the saved local account record and clears the uninstall feedback link. It does not delete your remote account or cancel a subscription.

PNG exports are ordinary files saved by Chrome. Downloaded files and Chrome's download records are controlled separately and can remain after uninstall. Delete downloaded copies using your file manager and download records through browser controls.

3. Permissions

  • activeTab: temporary access to the current page after you click the extension, to capture your chosen webpage content or selected text. Manual capture uses temporary access. Optional website permissions are requested one website at a time for remembered-region buttons.
  • scripting: inject the bundled capture adapter and temporary content picker into that page on demand.
  • storage: save preferences, trial usage, and account information locally, and keep temporary session drafts.
  • downloads: start local PNG downloads, including all pages of a long card. The extension does not use this permission to inspect unrelated download history.
  • Optional HTTP/HTTPS website access: after you allow a website, locally bundled scripts can detect matching regions on future visits and show a button when you hover. The extension does not request access to every website at once. You can revoke access in Chrome extension settings.

4. Sign-in and membership

Sign-in and checkout open https://crx.shiplang.com. Requests include the product ID, Chrome extension ID, extension version, browser interface language, and, when present, your account identifier and locally saved membership expiration. Account responses are accepted only from the expected ShipLang origin and service window, for this product. Card contents, post authors, and source links are not included in these requests.

ShipLang provides the account and payment infrastructure. Its integration guidance states that it retains the email, avatar, and membership expiration needed for the service until a deletion request, without retaining backups. To request remote deletion, contact support@shiplang.com with this extension's name or download link and your request; that guidance specifies processing within 7 days. The developer and ShipLang are independent; this extension does not represent ShipLang.

Google sign-in, when used, is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. Payment details are entered on the checkout provider's website and are not stored by this extension. The provider actually displayed at checkout determines the applicable terms: Stripe Privacy Policy or PayPro Global Privacy Policy. Consult checkout for actual prices, membership terms, payment options, and cancellation procedures.

After a signed-in popup is opened, the extension sets a one-time uninstall feedback URL containing the account identifier, interface language, and extension version. Chrome may open it on uninstall. Signing out from the popup clears that URL.

The Simplified Chinese interface has free exports. Other interface languages include 10 trial export batches; an active membership removes the export limit. A batch counts once after at least one PNG download is successfully started. If every download fails to start, no trial use is charged. A partially started batch counts once; downloads subsequently cancelled or interrupted by the browser still count. Upgrading or reopening the extension does not reset the trial count. Trial and membership information stays separate from card content.

5. Other external services

Opening external example, support, privacy, sign-in, checkout, or uninstall links causes normal requests to their destination services. Those services may process connection data such as IP address, browser information, and their own cookies. This policy is hosted on GitHub Pages; see the GitHub General Privacy Statement. Support emails are handled by the recipient and relevant email providers. Do not email passwords, access tokens, or private post content.

6. Purpose, sharing, and control

The extension does not include advertising, behavioral analytics, or tracking scripts. The developer does not sell card content or browsing information. Data is used to provide the card creation, download, account, and membership features described here. Use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Website content is not sent to an AI service.

You control which content is captured, edited, exported, and shared. The extension does not publish cards to social websites. You are responsible for checking accuracy, permissions, and attribution before sharing someone else's words.

7. Deletion and contact

Clear a card from the editor, close editor tabs, and end the browser session to remove session drafts. Clear extension data or uninstall to remove saved local preferences, trial state, and account data. Delete PNG files and browser download records separately. For remote account deletion, contact ShipLang as described above. For privacy questions about this extension, email rcchenfeng@gmail.com. For service assistance, email e@shiplang.com.

8. Changes

This policy will be updated if future versions change data handling. The effective date and extension version above identify the current policy.
